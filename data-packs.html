Scam shield

2026 fraud patterns our pack buyers should know — and what the packs do about them:

  • The Dormant Badge scam — scammers buy old defunct-factory Alibaba accounts to inherit the "Verified Supplier" badge. Counter: we don't trust platform badges — every pack row documents the supplier signals we could actually read (transaction history, ratings, audit badges, live-listing traceability), and we say plainly what we haven't verified.
  • Bait-and-switch metal — perfect sample, inferior alloys in the bulk order. Counter: the sample-first doctrine — one paid sample before scaling, escrow/Trade Assurance on bulk orders.
  • The Freight Forwarder Loophole — the factory refuses international shipping, forces you onto a Chinese forwarder, then ships scrap domestically. Counter: we favor suppliers with end-to-end international freight track records.
