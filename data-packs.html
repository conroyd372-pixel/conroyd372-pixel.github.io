📦 Data packs for builders

The raw intelligence behind this directory — structured catalog data for entrepreneurs, storefront builders, and sourcing tools.

What a data pack includes

  • Product records: titles, categories, price ranges, and listing metadata
  • Supplier data: names, ratings, and transaction history where documented
  • Fitment tags: chassis codes and vehicle fitment data
  • Material and specification data captured from the original listings
