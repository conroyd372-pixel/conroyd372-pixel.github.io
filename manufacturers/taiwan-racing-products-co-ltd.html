Taiwan Racing Products Co., Ltd.

Who makes racing safety gear? Taiwan Racing Products Co., Ltd. .

What they manufacture
racing safety equipment — FIA harnesses/seat belts, racing suits (FIA/SFI), racing seats, gloves, boots, underwear, steering wheels, window nets, helmet supports. OEM/ODM; claims production for top industry brands.
Verification
18 years on Alibaba; established 1999, Taiwan (Changhua)

Sample pricing seen: FIA harness RCU-1002H ~$80–100/set (MOQ 10); FIA racing suits ~$230–700. Major markets: Japan, US, Latin America, Germany, France, Australia.
